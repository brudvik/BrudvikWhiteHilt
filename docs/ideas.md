# 🗺️ Ideas for new content

Things that would fit the mod and are not built yet. They are ideas, not promises: nothing here is scheduled, and each one would get its own feature page when it is made. Back to the [main page](../README.MD) · [Documentation](README.md)

Everything below follows the [biome rule](../.github/copilot-instructions.md#game-balance-guidelines): content belongs to the biome where it is found, and a recipe never uses materials from a later biome.

##  Horns and companions

The [Dragon Horn](monsters.md#the-dragon-horn-a-white-dragon-at-your-side) calls a White Dragon, and the [Summoning Horn](difficulty.md) calls the black beasts.

- A horn for a different companion, for example a wolf pack on land or an escort for a ship.
- Letting the [dog](dog.md) and the dragon share progress with the [Saga](saga.md) and the [Bestiary](bestiary.md).
- More for the dog to do: new tricks and small jobs at home.

## ⚙️ Settings presets

Every setting can be changed, and there are many of them. The HUD already has a `Preset`. A preset for the whole mod (*Vanilla+*, *Standard*, *Hardcore*) would set many values at once in the [settings window](progression.md), with the server deciding.

## ⚓ More for the sea battles

Ideas that build on the [sea battles](sea-battles.md): the Nail Ships, their crew, the Ship Ballista, the boarding leap and the raid harbours.

**The fight**

- **More enemy tactics.** Nail Ships that ram, burning fire rafts drifting toward you, ambushes in fog, and Nail Ships that lie in wait along your saved routes.
- **The Kraken attacks everyone.** A Kraken that rises in the middle of a battle goes after the Nail Ship as well, so you can lure them into each other.

**Bigger enemies**

- **Naglfar as a world boss.** A huge ship with a draugr jarl on deck, called with its own horn. It drops Naglfar's nails, used for hull plating on your own ship.
- **Fleets of later biomes.** Ice ships in the Deep North and charred ships in the Ashlands sea, with that biome's crew and loot.

**Your own fleet**

- **Keep the prize.** Rebuild a captured Nail Ship at the Shipwright's Bench (nails and materials) into a Naglfar longship of your own.
- **A crew of the dead.** The Necromancer's Staff raises draugr that row, man the ballista or hold the deck. The dog comes along as a lookout and barks at sails.
- **Trophies on the ship.** The Nail Ship's dragon head mounted as your own figurehead, with a small bonus.

**On land and in harbour**

- **Harbour raids.** Nail Ships come for your jetties and mooring posts like a raid, met with the alarm bell, the oil cauldron and a shore ballista among the defences.
- **Fuller raid harbours.** A jarl guarding the hoard, huts and fires, a captive to free who becomes a trader, and waves of guards when the harbour's horn sounds.

**Navigation, saga and multiplayer**

- **A spyglass.** See sails far out before they see you, with the Lookout and Munin's Perch, and slip away if you want.
- **Fame at sea.** Saga titles such as *Sea-king* by the number of ships taken, and a bounty board for Nail Ships.
- **Wrecks and flotsam.** A sunk Nail Ship leaves flotsam that the fishing net or a boat hook picks up.
- **Holmgang at sea.** An agreed duel between two player ships, with its own saga deed.

## 🏛️ The world and its people

- **The Thing.** A thing stone where players vote on the laws of the server: blood moons, death penalty, PvP, a settings preset. A law that passes becomes a world modifier, which gives the settings presets above a place in the game.
- **Seasons and Fimbulwinter.** Seasons on the world clock: snow over the Meadows, frozen lakes you can walk on, ice fishing with the [net winch](fishing.md), a growing season for fields and [planting](planting.md), and now and then a long Fimbulwinter.
- **The Völva's prophecies.** A seeress reads the state of the world (biomes nobody has seen, bosses still alive, empty chests) and gives weekly prophecies that work as quests, paid in renown in the [Saga](saga.md).
- **Raven post.** Send letters and small parcels by raven to another player, also when they are offline. The raven flies across the map where everyone can see it, and can be shot down.
- **A living ecology.** Deer that migrate between biomes, wolves that hunt them, flocks of birds, and herds that thin out when they are hunted too hard.

## 📜 Saga and death

- **The skald.** The [Saga](saga.md) becomes alliterative verse from templates. A slain boss raises a runestone with the poem by itself, and a skald recites it in the mead hall.
- **Kin and inheritance.** A permadeath mode: when a hero dies for good, a descendant takes over with an heirloom, part of the skills and the same saga. A family tree on the [School Bench](school.md).
- **Hel's road.** Instead of only a tombstone, you walk a short road through Hel as a ghost and can fight your way back closer to your grave. Optional, decided by the server.

## ✨ Magic, music and places

- **Rune magic you compose.** Carve three runes on a stave (Thurisaz, Kenaz and Algiz, for example) and the combination gives a spell. Each rune belongs to the biome its material comes from, and spells you find are written in the saga.
- **Musicians.** A lur, a goat horn and a harp that players can play together, in step over the network. Music in the hall gives a rested bonus.
- **Dwarven mines.** Generated mine tunnels under the Mountains, with dwarven forges, scrolls and cave-ins: a dungeon of their own that belongs to that biome.

## 🧑‍💻 For modders and streamers

- **Code inspector.** An overlay in the game that shows who owns a ZDO, the RPCs as they are sent and which Harmony patch just ran, with a link to the source file on GitHub. It fits a mod that is written to be learned from.
- **The audience decides the evening.** Twitch or YouTube chat votes on events (a blood moon, the weather, a black beast), building on the White Hilt videos.
