## 🌱 PLANTING

The cultivator (vanilla and White Hilt) can plant the berry bushes, mushrooms, flowers, debris and decorative flora you find in the world. Each one unlocks once you have had one of every item it costs. They are the vanilla plants, so they grow back, give and look exactly as in the wild, and players without the mod can see and pick them.

| Plant | Costs |
|-------|-------|
| Raspberry, Blueberry, Cloudberry and Lingonberry bush | 5 of the berry |
| Mushrooms, Yellow and Blue mushrooms | 5 of the mushroom |
| Thistle, Dandelion, Smoke puffs | 5 of it |
| Fiddlehead fern | 15 Fiddlehead |
| Branch, Stone, Flint (debris) | 5 Wood, 1 Stone, 5 Flint |
| Small beech | 1 Beech Seeds |
| Small fir, Small dead fir | 1 Fir Cone |
| Bush, Heath bush, Shrub, Heath shrub, Vines, Ashlands fern | 2 Wood |
| Plains bush | 3 Wood |
| Small Yggdrasil shoot | 1 Yggdrasil Wood, 2 Wood |
| Ancient sapling (grows into a swamp tree) | 1 Ancient Seed |
| Autumn birch sapling (grows into an autumn birch) | 1 Birch Seeds |
| Yggdrasil sapling (grows into a Yggdrasil shoot), off by default | 1 Sap |
| Ashwood sapling (grows into an Ashlands tree), off by default | 1 Beech Seeds, 1 Sulfur Stone |

The saplings are copies of the vanilla birch sapling dressed in the bark and leaves of their tree, and grow in every biome unless `SaplingsAnyBiome` is off. Each has its own section under `Planting.Saplings` (on/off, growth time, tree size, grow radius). They use the same names as PlantEverything's saplings, so saplings planted with that mod keep growing.

Aim at a berry bush, mushroom, flower or piece of debris with a cultivator and press the remove key (middle mouse) to remove it; it is picked first. By default this works on wild ones too, and planted decorative flora stays. The hammer cannot remove them. The `Planting` section lets the server switch planting, debris or flora off, require cultivated ground, give back the cost when removing, allow removing wild plants or planted flora, stop enemies attacking planted things and turn vine snapping off. `Planting.Costs` sets each cost; 0 leaves the plant out. Planting is off while PlantEverything is installed.
